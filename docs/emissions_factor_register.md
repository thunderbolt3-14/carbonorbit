## Entry requirements

Before entering a factor, record its original source URL, publication year, geography, vehicle and fuel, pollutant, unit, occupancy basis, and boundary (tailpipe, operational, or lifecycle). The bus and private-mode factors must have compatible units and boundaries. Convert units in a recorded calculation, not by silently mixing factors.

TERI's Delhi g CO2 per passenger-kilometre estimates are contextual examples, not BMTC factors. ITF's lifecycle results must not be mixed with tailpipe-only values. CSTEP national scenario reductions are not emission factors and do not establish that ETA information causes mode shift. If no comparable factors can be sourced, leave the emissions calculator operational but do not report a numerical carbonOrbit scenario.

The scenario calculator requires factors and source descriptions in its request; it does not fall back to fabricated defaults. This closes the current evaluation's decision gate without asserting that emissions calculations or benefits were measured. Reopen the gate only when compatible, traceable factors and explicit activity/diversion inputs are approved.
