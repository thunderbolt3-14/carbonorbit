# carbonOrbit Citation Audit

**Audit date:** 21 November 2026 (metadata closeout)

**Purpose:** verify source identity and bibliographic metadata used in the literature survey and report; document access limits and prevent claims from exceeding the available evidence.

## Audit standard

Publisher, repository, DOI, and institutional records were preferred over secondary citation pages. “Metadata checked” means the title/author/date and available journal or record details were compared with a primary record. It does not mean the full paper was read. Full-text claims should only be used where the team has retained and reviewed the full text. ## Corrections and consistency findings

1. **Primary versus abstract evidence:** Kumar et al. (2025), Brakewood & Watkins (2019), and the cited Brakewood/Tang studies have verified publisher metadata and abstract-level claim boundaries; their full article texts were not reviewed. Amogha et al. (2026) and the ICCIKE 2025 paper remain full-text access checks. Keep detailed claims out unless those full texts are obtained and reviewed.
2. **TCD thesis URL:** use the full dissertation PDF, not the supplied abstract PDF. Preserve the table-versus-prose MAE discrepancy in the notes.
3. **Two Mansurova-related sources:** the 2025 *Data* paper describes a GPS-to-GTFS dataset; the 2025 ICCIKE paper is a distinct travel-time prediction paper. Cite separately.
4. **Wills et al.:** use the arXiv preprint’s actual bus-GHG/GPS scope; it is not evidence about passenger preference or modal shift. No later publication is cited in this report.
5. **Emissions boundary:** the TERI Delhi comparison, ITF lifecycle assessment, CSTEP national scenarios, and historical state inventory measure different quantities. No values from them were inserted as carbonOrbit operating factors.
6. **Sample and validation claims:** project results in the report are generated simulation outputs and should always carry that qualifier. None of the cited studies validates those project metrics.
7. **Access date:** the publisher/proceedings/DOI metadata follow-up for the sources above was performed on 21 November 2026. Before formal submission, preserve the access date for any additional pages or full texts the team actually consults.

## Final citation readiness

The named metadata checks in the table are closed, including the IEEE bibliographic fields, the Brakewood/Tang publisher records, and the ATRF proceedings PDF. The report's relevant statements are restricted to publisher abstracts where full papers were not reviewed. Remaining full-text checks are optional for this bounded submission unless the team wants to make detailed claims from those sources; Amogha and ICCIKE claims must stay abstract/record-level. No citation metadata item in the user's listed set is left open.
