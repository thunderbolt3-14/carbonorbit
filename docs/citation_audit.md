# carbonOrbit Citation Audit

**Audit date:** 20 November 2026

**Purpose:** verify source identity and bibliographic metadata used in the literature survey and report; document access limits and prevent claims from exceeding the available evidence.

## Audit standard

Publisher, repository, DOI, and institutional records were preferred over secondary citation pages. “Metadata checked” means the title/author/date and available journal or record details were compared with a primary record. It does not mean the full paper was read. Full-text claims should only be used where the team has retained and reviewed the full text. ## Corrections and consistency findings

1. **Primary versus abstract evidence:** Kumar et al. (2025), Amogha et al. (2026), and the ICCIKE 2025 paper remain limited to records/abstracts unless the team obtains and checks full text. Their result statements must be labeled accordingly.
2. **TCD thesis URL:** use the full dissertation PDF, not the supplied abstract PDF. Preserve the table-versus-prose MAE discrepancy in the notes.
3. **Two Mansurova-related sources:** the 2025 *Data* paper describes a GPS-to-GTFS dataset; the 2025 ICCIKE paper is a distinct travel-time prediction paper. Cite separately.
4. **Wills et al.:** use the arXiv preprint’s actual bus-GHG/GPS scope; it is not evidence about passenger preference or modal shift. Later publication status remains to be checked.
5. **Emissions boundary:** the TERI Delhi comparison, ITF lifecycle assessment, CSTEP national scenarios, and historical state inventory measure different quantities. No values from them were inserted as carbonOrbit operating factors.
6. **Sample and validation claims:** project results in the report are generated simulation outputs and should always carry that qualifier. None of the cited studies validates those project metrics.
7. **Access date:** when preparing the submitted reference list, record the date the team actually accessed each final PDF or publisher page. This audit date is 20 November 2026.

## Final citation readiness

The report bibliography is ready for a project submission draft, with access limits marked. A strict scholarly reference freeze still requires the outstanding checks in the table—especially title-page transcription for the thesis, complete journal metadata for secondary pages, full-text review for abstract-only conference/review sources, and later-publication checks for preprints. Do not remove these qualifications unless those source checks are completed.
